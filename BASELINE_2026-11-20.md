## Interpretation and limitations

This is an initial passive observation, not a controlled idle baseline: user activity, display, radios, background services, battery/AC state, fan state, and ambient temperature were not controlled or logged. The temperatures are kernel-exposed zones; their physical meaning is driver/platform-specific. The data does not demonstrate an ATOCIA energy saving or validate a thermal model.

## Next experiment

Acquire a repeatable baseline with documented AC/battery state, display brightness, ambient temperature, fan policy, and background workload. Then perform a short, moderate, monitored CPU workload with a conservative stop condition and compare the transient response. Do not change firmware, power limits, voltage, or fan controls during the initial characterization.
